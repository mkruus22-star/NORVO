What is a bioclimatic pergola?

A bioclimatic pergola is an aluminium patio roof whose louvres can be rotated from 0 to 100°. With the louvres closed the roof is rainproof and water drains through hidden gutters and the posts. With the louvres open, light and fresh air reach the terrace, so you can adjust shade, sun and ventilation to the weather.

Which pergola models does NORVO offer?

NORVO has three models. N2 has manual louvre control and needs no electricity (3 standard sizes). N4 has motorised louvres and perimeter LED lighting (5 standard sizes). N7 is a made-to-order ultra-premium model up to 400 × 800 cm with integrated louvre LED lighting, a rain sensor and optional motorised insect screens and heaters.

What sizes are available?

Standard sizes are 300 × 300, 400 × 300, 600 × 300, 400 × 400 and 600 × 400 cm, and all standard models are 275 cm high. Custom sizes are also available to order.

Can the pergola be mounted to a house wall?

Yes. The pergola works both as a free-standing structure in the garden and wall-mounted to the house, where it extends the living space outdoors.

Does a NORVO pergola withstand the Estonian climate?

Yes. NORVO pergolas are designed specifically for the Baltic climate. The frame is certified 6063-T6 anodised, powder-coated aluminium that resists corrosion and fading, and the structure is built to withstand strong wind and snow loads.

Which colours are available?

The standard colour is anthracite grey (RAL 7016). Brown and white are available as custom orders.

Which accessories can be added?

The pergola can be fitted with ZIP screens (blinds), frameless or framed sliding glass doors, sliding louvre doors and fixed louvre doors. The N7 model can also have heaters and motorised insect screens.

Do you deliver and install across Estonia?

Yes. We deliver pergolas across Estonia and provide professional installation, so you get the complete solution from one place.

Do you also help with the terrace and electrical work?

Yes. If your terrace needs renovating or adjusting so the pergola fits, we do the work. We also handle the electrical work for the pergola’s lighting, motors and accessories, so you can order everything from one place.
