Data protection

Privacy policy

Last updated 20.11.2026

We take care of your data. This page explains which personal data we collect through the NORVO website norvo.ee, why we use it, who we share it with and what rights you have.

1. Who is responsible for your data

The controller of your personal data is Lavrek Solutions OÜ, operating under the brand NORVO Outdoor Living.

  • Registry code: 16245465
  • Address: Viljandi mnt 74, 79514 Rapla, Estonia
  • Email: info@norvo.ee
  • Phone: +372 5345 0781

For any data protection questions, write to info@norvo.ee.

2. What data we collect

Enquiry form. Name, email address, phone number, city, preferred pergola model, size, mounting type and comment.

Pergola configurator. Name, email address, phone number, location, comment and the pergola configuration you chose, together with the quote created for it.

Communication with us. Data you share with us by email, phone or social media.

Technical data. When you visit the website, our hosting and security providers process technical data such as your IP address, browser type and time of visit. This is used only to run and protect the website.

3. Why we use your data and on what legal basis

  • Preparing a quote and answering your enquiry – steps taken at your request before entering into a contract (GDPR Art. 6(1)(b)).
  • Performing the contract – delivery, installation, warranty and customer support (GDPR Art. 6(1)(b)).
  • Legal obligations such as accounting (GDPR Art. 6(1)(c)).
  • Running and securing the website – legitimate interest (GDPR Art. 6(1)(f)).

We do not send you marketing emails without your separate consent and we never sell your data.

4. Who we share data with

We use trusted service providers that process data on our behalf and only on our instructions:

  • Cloudflare, Inc. – website hosting and security.
  • Resend – sending quote emails (European Union servers).
  • Google – email service (Google Workspace) and website fonts (Google Fonts).
  • FormSubmit – forwarding enquiry form data to our email.
  • jsDelivr – delivering the software libraries for the configurator's 3D view and PDF creation.
  • ESTO AS – only if you choose to pay by hire purchase or in instalments.

We may also share data with an installation partner when needed to fulfil your order, and with authorities when required by law.

5. Transfers outside the European Union

Some of the providers above (such as Cloudflare, Google and FormSubmit) may process data outside the European Economic Area, including in the United States. In that case your data is protected by a European Commission adequacy decision (EU-US Data Privacy Framework) or by standard contractual clauses approved by the European Commission.

6. How long we keep data

  • Enquiries and quotes that did not lead to a contract – up to 2 years, so we can return to your quote if needed.
  • Customer and contract data – until the end of the contract and warranty period, and then as long as needed to handle possible claims.
  • Accounting source documents – 7 years, as required by the Estonian Accounting Act.
  • Technical logs – for a short period, according to the provider's terms.

7. Cookies

Our website does not use advertising, tracking or analytics cookies. Our hosting and security provider may use strictly necessary technical cookies to protect the website from attacks and malicious bots.

8. Your rights

You have the right to:

  • access the data we hold about you;
  • have inaccurate data corrected;
  • have your data erased when we no longer have a reason to keep it;
  • restrict the processing of your data;
  • object to processing based on legitimate interest;
  • receive the data you gave us in a machine-readable format (data portability).

To use your rights, write to info@norvo.ee. We reply within one month.

If you believe we have breached your data protection rights, you can contact the Estonian Data Protection Inspectorate (info@aki.ee) or a court.

9. Changes

We may update this privacy policy from time to time, for example when we add a new service. The current version is always on this page, with its date shown at the top.
